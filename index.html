Data & privacy

Data & privacy

Saved property names, coordinates, notes, and wind settings stay in this browser on this device. They are not synced to CSO or uploaded to a CSO server. Clearing Safari website data or changing devices can erase saved locations.

When you load weather, your coordinates are sent to the National Weather Service. When the map is displayed, map tiles reveal the approximate map area to OpenStreetMap or USGS, depending on the selected layer. Place search sends the search text to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. No analytics or advertising scripts are included.

Install on iPhone
  1. Open the secure CSO website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Tap Add. Open CSO from the new Home Screen icon.

Installation requires CSO to be hosted over HTTPS. This project folder by itself is not a live website.
