Data, backups & privacy

Saved property names, coordinates, notes, and wind settings stay in this browser on this device. They are not synced to CSO or uploaded to a CSO server. Export a backup before clearing browser data or changing devices. Backup files contain precise hunting locations—keep them private.

When you load weather, your coordinates are sent to the National Weather Service. When the map is displayed, the requested map tiles reveal the approximate map area to the USGS map service. No analytics or advertising scripts are included.

Install on iPhone
  1. Open the secure CSO website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Tap Add. Open CSO from the new Home Screen icon.

Installation requires CSO to be hosted over HTTPS. This project folder by itself is not a live website.
